\acknowledgements
They say that what matters most is not the destination but the journey,
everything that happens along the way, the people we meet, the knowledge we gain, the good and the bad moments. That is what defines us and what we carry with us for the rest of our lives.
This is far from the end; it is only one more journey coming to its conclusion,
and I want to use this space to honour everyone who walked it with me.

First, the place where I grew academically over these years, the Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Lisboa (ISEL),
and in particular the people who tirelessly make this institution a place where it is a pleasure and a source of pride to study. A special thank you to my supervisors, Professors José Simão and Filipe Freitas, for their
availability, their help and their advice throughout this year. A supervisor's word is like a lantern in the forest: it does not shorten the path, but it lights up the obstacles.
A word of thanks also to my colleague Nuno Bartolomeu, who was a great help and support throughout these years of the master's.

Second, my thanks to Santander Portugal, and especially to the Enterprise Architecture team,
who opened the doors to the banking sector and to the professional world. I can only thank them for the excellent welcome,
for the support they give me and for the help they have given me all this time. Wisdom and knowledge do not fall into our laps; we have to
listen and open our hearts to learn from those with more experience. A special thank you to my mentor, Bernardo Cardoso,
for his availability, patience, advice, fun and help.

To my friends, for being themselves: people I can trust, who will never let me down and who will always
be by my side.

To my family, for supporting me, and especially to my parents, for the investment and the love that give me strength,
and that made me the person I am today, a person I can be proud of.

Finally, thank you to my girlfriend, Mariana, for inspiring me to be better than I was yesterday,
for being a light in the darkness, for being my confidante, for being herself. I do not know which words are best
to express what you are to me, how you change me, how you influence me, they are small words that
cannot describe the immense impact you have on my world. Having you by my side makes me whole, and this work would never
have been possible without you. The good moments bring us together, the bad ones unite us, and to you I can only say thank you for being my
right hand, my heart, my friend, my girlfriend.
